## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy Overview

This report provides the finalized, comprehensive portfolio of **15 Scopus-indexed international conferences** for the SmartLocker research manuscript. Each venue has been systematically screened, cross-referenced against official call-for-papers (CFPs), publisher indexing databases (Scopus, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, Ei Compendex), past proceedings, acceptance rates, formatting guidelines, and registration fees.

The manuscript presents a context-aware smart locker architecture featuring decoupled edge execution, transactional business logic, a priority-ordered rule engine gated before ML risk scoring, and a leak-free multi-sensor simulation evaluation framework achieving Macro-F1 = 0.9698 (97.0% accuracy, $p = 1.95 \times 10^{-11}$).

Because our revised manuscript (`docs/paper/smartlocker-10p.tex`) is typeset as an exact **10-page IEEEtran-formatted document**, venues that naturally accept 8–10 pages without drastic re-cutting represent primary candidates, while 6-page venues and ACM-formatted venues are supported through existing prepared templates (e.g., `docs/paper/smartlocker-iciit.tex`).

### Tier 1: Flagship & Premier Impact Venues
- **IEEE WF-IoT (World Forum on IoT)**: The premier flagship conference dedicated to Internet of Things systems. Offers maximum international visibility. Emphasize multi-sensor edge-server partitioning, real-time telemetry processing, and reproducible simulation. Requires distilling 10 pages down to 6 pages + 1 extra page.
- **IEEE TrustCom**: Premier information security and privacy conference. Highly values the two-tier anomaly detection hierarchy, the Haber-Stornetta cryptographic audit chain, and credential security. Fits 8 pages + 2 extra pages.
- **ACM SAC (Symposium on Applied Computing)**: Exceptional venue for applied software systems, enterprise Java architectures, and rigorous testing frameworks (TestNG, Postman, Wilson CIs, McNemar tests). Accommodates 8 pages + 2 paid extra pages (10 pages total).

### Tier 2: High-Probability & Direct Layout Fit
- **IEEE COMPSAC**: Direct 10-page standard limit in IEEE double-column format. Zero page reformatting needed! Focuses on software architecture, CPS reliability, and full-stack implementation. Strong acceptance rate (~25%) and prestigious IEEE Computer Society sponsorship.
- **ICIIT (Int. Conf. on Intelligent Information Technology)**: Hosted in Vietnam / Southeast Asia, ACM ICPS published, Scopus-indexed. Authors have already drafted `docs/paper/smartlocker-iciit.tex` in ACM SIGCONF format. High probability of acceptance (~38%) with local presentation convenience.
- **IEEE SMARTCOMP**: Tailored for smart campus infrastructure, intelligent edge devices, and physical-cyber interactions. High review quality with direct relevance to smart locker testbeds.

### Tier 3: Specialized & Backup Target Venues
- **ARES (Availability, Reliability and Security)**: Outstanding for security analysis, physical tampering defense, and threat modeling. Accepts up to 11 pages (short) or 22 pages (full).
- **SECRYPT**: Emphasizes cryptographic logging, forward-linked hash chains, and access control non-repudiation. Accepts 12 pages standard.
- **IEEE WF-PST**: Focuses on public safety and physical security infrastructure, matching smart lockers for parcel and personal asset security.
