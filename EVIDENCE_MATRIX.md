### Per-Class F1-Score Breakdown
| Class | Baseline B0 F1 | Proposed B1 F1 | Improvement Rationale |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **Normal Authorized** | 0.200 | **0.788** | B1 cross-validates presence, door close, and steady weight |

### Statistical Significance (McNemar's Test)
- **Contingency Matrix**:
  - $n_{00}$ (Both B0 and B1 incorrect): 7
  - $n_{01}$ (B0 correct, B1 incorrect): 0
  - $n_{10}$ (B1 correct, B0 incorrect): 25
  - $n_{11}$ (Both B0 and B1 correct): 68
- **Chi-Square Statistic ($\chi^2$)**: $23.04$ (with continuity correction)
- **p-value**: $2.0 \times 10^{-6}$ ($p < 0.001$, highly statistically significant)
- **Conclusion**: The detection superiority of the multi-sensor architecture B1 over baseline B0 is statistically conclusive and not attributable to chance.
