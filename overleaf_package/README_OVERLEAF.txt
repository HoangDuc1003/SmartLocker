HOW TO USE IN OVERLEAF:
1. Log in to your Overleaf account (https://www.overleaf.com).
2. Click 'New Project' -> 'Upload Project'.
3. Select 'SmartLocker_Paper_Overleaf.zip' and upload.
4. Settings:
   - Compiler: pdfLaTeX (recommended default) or XeLaTeX
   - TeX Live version: 2022 / 2023 / 2024 (any modern version)
   - Main document: main.tex
5. Click 'Recompile'. The PDF will be compiled cleanly with all figures,
   correct Times fonts, and complete references with DOIs!
